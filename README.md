**UK Road Accident Analysis**

No matter where we live, traffic accidents happen often in our area, and I wanted to understand the patterns behind them.

This project analyzes UK road accident data to uncover when, where, and why accidents occur.

The dataset had timestamps, so I broke them down by hour and created time periods (morning, afternoon, evening, night).
I also dropped records missing critical info like location coordinates.The analysis threw up some interesting contradictions.
Most accidents happen on dry roads in fine weather – which makes sense because that's when most driving happens.
But the heatmap showing road surface versus weather conditions revealed some dangerous combos, like wet roads even after rain stops.

The interactive Folium map was honestly the most fun part. Watching accident hotspots appear as red blobs on a real map makes the data feel tangible. You can zoom in and see exactly which intersections are problematic.
The speed limit chart surprised me – 30 mph roads account for way more accidents than highways, probably because they're where cars, pedestrians, and cyclists all mix together.
And motorcycles? They show up in the top vehicle types way more than their numbers on the road would suggest – definitely vulnerable road users.
